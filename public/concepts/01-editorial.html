Dictionary entry · ed. 01

click /klɪk/ noun · verb

1. A quiet burst of yes between two people in the same room.

e.g. “we clicked over the bread course.”

2. The reason a stranger becomes a friend.

see also: low pressure, familiar by week 3, slow dating.

3. verb. To privately tap a person you would like to see again.

Mutual is an unlocked second event. No mutual, and nobody knows.
