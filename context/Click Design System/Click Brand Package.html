03 - Construction & clear space

Give it room

Minimum clear space on all sides equals the height of the “c” (marked x). Never crowd the mark with type, edges, or other logos.

Alignment. The sparkle pair sits optically above the i-stem and leans up-right. Don't recentre, resize, or recolour it independently of the wordmark.

04 - Minimum sizes & favicon

Holds from 16px up

The c mark stays legible down to a 16px favicon. Below 90px wide, drop the wordmark and use the icon alone.

Rule of thumb. Wordmark minimum: 90px wide on screen, 22mm in print. Icon minimum: 16px.

05 - Colour

Three colours, no gradients

Deep purple grounds the brand, cream is the canvas, and lavender is reserved - it appears only as the spark. Restraint is the point.

~70% use

Support shade. #2F2566 (deeper purple) is allowed for depth - shadows, pressed states, secondary grounds - but it is not a brand colour in its own right.

06 - Typography

Poppins, throughout

One geometric family carries the whole system. SemiBold for the logotype and headlines, Medium for subheads, Regular for body. Tight tracking on display, normal on text.
