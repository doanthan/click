# Click — The Click Mechanic (full coordination flow, web-only)

This is **the feature** — give it the most craft. It must look like a funded Silicon-Valley product: clean, warm, delightful in the small moments, dead-easy to understand. **Web only** — the claude-design version was app-native; strip all native chrome.

**Source of truth (don't deviate):** `21_CLICK_MECHANIC.md` (Parts A+B) + `TECH/CLICK_LIFECYCLE_PROCESS_MAP.md` (the state machine). **Copy:** `CLICK_LANGUAGE.md` locked strings. **Explainer:** link to the How Click Works page wherever a moment could confuse.

**The two guardrails that make this magic, not slop:**
1. **Don't give away the magic.** Show *outcomes* and one warm line — never the internal rules (timers, caps, anonymity internals, ranking). If a moment needs more, link "How clicking works →". The intrigue is the retention.
2. **Cute, but professional.** Micro-animations are purposeful (100–300ms, gentle easing, reduced-motion respected). The ✨ spark is the one signature flourish, used sparingly at the three peak moments (mutual, both-going, connected). No confetti dumps, no bounce, no looping motion.

=== 🔴 INTENT-NEUTRAL — it must NOT read as a dating app (the #1 brand risk; research 27 Jun, friend-making) ===
Research finding: the dating-app FEELING comes from the FORMAT (profile/photo-first, swiping, instant matches, chat), not mainly the words — and the apps that work for FRIENDSHIP are ACTIVITY-FIRST ("a tiny commitment to show up replaces the long performance of a profile"; friendships form through repeated low-stakes contact in a shared context, "none of those hours in a chat"). Click is STRUCTURALLY the friend-making model — lean into it, don't drift to the dating playbook:
- **Activity-first, not profile-first.** You meet by showing up to a real event, NOT by judging photos/bios. No swiping; no photo-first judgment (no bio/age on the card, anonymous-until-mutual); NO chat (you meet at a real activity). This is the moat — keep it loud. It's WHY Click isn't a dating app.
- **Foreground friends + activities + networking EQUALLY.** In every daily set / example, the majority of intents shown are "Here for friends" / "Here for the activities" / "Growing my circle" / "Networking". "Open to dating" is ONE of several, NEVER the default, the majority, or a headline. (Rule of thumb: of any 3-person set, ≤1 is dating.)
- **The mutual reveal leads with the CONNECTION + the shared thing — never "a match."** "You clicked with [Name]" + the intent line + "find something you'd both enjoy and meet there." NEVER "it's a match", hearts/💕, or "singles" (except the gated aggregate romantic FOMO, dating-viewers only). The ✨ is brand, not romance.
- **No dating-app signifiers anywhere** — no hearts, no match/like/swipe, no romantic copy, no coupley framing. "Open to dating" is a desire a user opted into, shown intent-neutrally — never the product's frame.
- **The quiz + interests + clusters** (Timeleft-style) are the friend-making engine — lean on them.
🔴 **THE TEST (run on every screen):** would a friends-ONLY user feel this is for them? If anything reads date-y — a heart, a "match", dating foregrounded, a coupley two-people-merging visual — fix it.

=== THE FLOW (end-to-end — it must feel like ONE seamless thread, not 12 separate pages) ===
click → wait → MUTUAL (notify + surface) → propose a plan → they agree → both RSVP → both going ✨ → attend → post-event click → connected. Plus the calm recovery/terminal branches. The user always knows where they are and what's next; every step answers "what now?" with ONE action (research: complete the core action in <2 taps). Map:
**1. CLICK** (anonymous, optimistic "clicked") → **2. WAIT** (private, never shown to them) → **3. MUTUAL ✨** (the peak — see below) → **4. PROPOSE** (Click's pick OR your own — 🔴 NOT a booking) → **5. THEY AGREE** (the other says "Save my spot") → **6. BOTH RSVP** (then, and only then, both book — propose ≠ book, 21 §B5) → **7. BOTH GOING ✨** (locked in + add to calendar) → **8. ATTEND** → **9. POST-EVENT CLICK** (the loop can begin again) → **10. CONNECTED** ("We clicked 👍").

=== WHEN & WHERE THE MUTUAL APPEARS (the dopamine moment — get it RIGHT) ===
A mutual exists the **instant the second person clicks** (detected in the send-click transaction — `21`; no delay, no cron). It must be FAST and unmissable (research: a delayed match notification kills the reward):
- **On a click surface when it happens** (click page · Who-was-there · the profile modal) → the **mutual-reveal MODAL fires live** over the page (§B).
- **Away / on another page** → an **immediate notification**: the in-app notification bell lights (a calm dot, not a flood) + an optional push **"It's mutual — you clicked with [Name]. ✨"** (locked copy). Opening it → the reveal modal.
- **It also SURFACES warmly where you'll see it next:**
   · **Dashboard (Mode B):** a gentle **"✨ you clicked with [Name] — suggest a plan →"** moment near the top (below the post-event prompt, above the feed) — the single warmest thing on the dashboard; opens the reveal/coordination. ONE at a time, never a stack.
   · **Your clicks → Live mutuals** (§E3) — the durable home; the mutual rests here as "open" until you propose.
   · The **activity feed** gets a quiet line ("✨ You clicked with [Name].").
- **Positive-only, anonymity holds:** you are NEVER notified about a one-way click (no "someone clicked you", no "likes you" queue) — only the mutual, only the outcome. Warm, opportunity-framed, ONE calm signal — never an urgency/FOMO flood.
