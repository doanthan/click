## PROMPT (paste everything below)

You are Click's senior product designer. Redesign the **How It Works** page of the Click website to the copy and rules below. Click is a responsive **website** (mobile-optimised), not a native app. Click gets people out doing real-life activities together; the activity is the reason to show up, and connection is the bonus. This is **not** a dating app — it's intent-neutral (friends first).

### Brand lock (do not deviate)
- **Palette:** Cream `#F9F6F0` canvas · Deep Purple `#3B2F81` (primary/selected, flat) · Lavender `#C8B8F8` (small accents/hairlines only, never a section canvas) · `--lavender-wash` `#F0ECF4` (large soft fills, e.g. the THE CLICK band) · Ink `#1C1830` (headings/body) · Slate `#6B6580` (eyebrows/secondary) · Mist `#E8E4F0` / Mist-strong `#DDD7EA` (tag hairlines).
- **Type:** Poppins SemiBold for the wordmark, display headlines, section headings and primary-button labels; system-font stack for body. Body 16/1.5, generous ~8pt spacing rhythm.
- **✨:** at most ONE sparkle on the entire page — a single Deep-Purple `#3B2F81` glyph, on the "It's mutual" card only, TRAILING. Never the multicolour/orange emoji. Never elsewhere.
- **Punctuation:** hyphens, never em-dashes.
- **Layout:** one app container (~1100–1200px, ~24–32px gutters); each section ≈ one viewport; let it breathe. Mobile = full-width single column. Pre-login accessible (no auth wall).
- **Imagery:** warm, real-life **activity** photography, warm-graded — clay-covered hands mid-throw, runners on a street at dawn, a long lunch table, cocktails being poured. Faces soft / incidental / cropped. NEVER stock "diverse people laughing", never 3D/blobs/illustrations-of-people. 1–2 images max (a hero moment + one in THE MODEL or THE CLICK). The two THE CLICK cards stay stylised, not real product chrome.

### Voice rules (binding — CLICK_LANGUAGE §5b/§5c)
- **Lead by desire + science, never by "a feeling."** No "chemistry/spark/a feeling/hunch."
- **The click is a by-product ("THE BONUS"),** teased and kept curious — never explain the mechanism.
- **No rejection angle, ever** — don't plant the could-be-no AND don't name the safety ("no one gets turned down", "nothing is rated"). Let safety be felt.
- **Never label the reader** (no "shy / introverts / lonely / anxious / find your tribe"). The reframe "it was the rooms that disappeared, not you" does the welcoming.
- **Friends-first**, dating last and light, every intent equal weight; state intent-neutrality positively (never "not a dating app").
- **"Room"** = the science metaphor + the physical space only, never the activity label.
- CTA is **"Request an invite"**. Post-event opener is **"Did you click with anyone?"**.

### Page copy (implement verbatim)

Headline (two lines, largest display moment):
> The best people you'll meet this year
> aren't on an app. They're across the room.
Sub: "Click gets you out doing the things you love, in real life - and the people you'll click with are already there."

**WHY IT WORKS** — eyebrow `WHY IT WORKS`
Heading: "You don't click with a profile. You click in person."
Body: "Real connection was never built through a screen. It happens in person - side by side, doing something you both love, more than once. That's how the best friendships start, and plenty more besides. Psychologists call it the proximity effect. We just call it showing up."
Follow-on: "The best friendships mostly started the same way: the same place, the same people - school, uni, a team, a job. Not effort. Just proximity, on repeat. Those rooms get rarer as life fills up. It was the rooms that disappeared, not you. Click brings them back."
Second beat: "It starts smaller than people think. One thing in common - the same band, the same hobby, the same way of seeing a thing - and the rest fills in: you're a bit like me. Do that thing in person, and you connect in a way a screen never can. It's how people have always found their people."

**THE MODEL** — eyebrow `THE MODEL` — three steps (icon + heading + one line):
1. "Find your thing - or let it find you" — "Pottery in Newtown, a sunrise run in Marrickville, cocktails in Surry Hills. Real places, near you, this week. Pick something you'd actually do, or let Click point you toward one you'll love, alongside people you'd probably get on with. Either way, the activity is the reason to show up."
2. "Show up" — "This is the part that does the quiet work. The best friendships aren't found, they're formed - and everyone here chose the same thing you did. That's common ground before you've said a word."
3. "The night was always the point" — "You came for something good, and you got it. Click with someone? Even better. If not, you still did something you love. **Show up, and everything else is a bonus.**"

**THE CLICK** — eyebrow `THE BONUS` — this is the emotional peak: most breathing room, set on a `--lavender-wash` band.
Heading: "And every so often, you just click with someone."
Body: "Do the things you love, around the same people, and now and then one of them turns out to be someone you just click with - no effort. The same band, the same odd sense of humour, the same reason for being there. Sometimes it's a stranger across the room; sometimes it's someone Click thought you'd get on with. Either way, it's the oldest way friendships start, and it still works. Click keeps it quiet until it's mutual, then helps you do the next thing together. Not with everyone, which is exactly what makes it worth something."
Intent line: "A new friend, your regular crew, your people for the thing you love - and sometimes something more. It all works the same."
Two small stylised cards beside the copy: Card 1 "Did you click with anyone?" / "A quiet question after the event." — Card 2 "It's mutual ✨" / "The good part: you both clicked." (the page's only ✨, trailing, Deep-Purple).
